ABOUT THE PROJECT

HALOS

AI-ASSISTED RESEARCH PROTOTYPE

Dietary Salt Intake Assessment & Prediction System

HALOS investigates whether combining 24-hour dietary recall with habitual monthly food-consumption patterns can improve machine-learning-based estimation of daily dietary salt intake.

PRIVACY

Prototype data policy

Browser localStorage is suitable only for demonstration/prototyping and must not be used for identifiable patient health information in production. Use anonymous study IDs and avoid unnecessary personal identifiers.

HALOS does not provide medical diagnosis, medication advice, or individualized treatment recommendations.
